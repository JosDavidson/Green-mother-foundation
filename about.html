Who we are

Green Mother Foundation is dedicated to accessibility, social empowerment, independent living, livelihood development and community progress.

We focus particularly on persons with physical challenges, working towards a society in which people can reach public spaces, essential services, education, employment and entrepreneurship with greater independence and dignity.

Meaningful empowerment takes more than temporary help. It takes practical accessibility, relevant skills, supportive communities, and the chance for each person to decide their own future.

Our wider community work introduces rural youth to emerging technologies and digital awareness. We work with educational institutions, employers, community organisations, government bodies and technology partners so that what we start lasts.

Our story

Building a society where everyone can take part.

Registered in 2013 as a public charitable trust in Kattumannarkoil, Green Mother Foundation serves persons with physical disabilities, children and youth from rural families, and women seeking skills and livelihoods. It is guided by the belief that every person deserves respect, opportunity and the freedom to pursue their aspirations.

Physical barriers, limited access to resources and a lack of suitable opportunities can affect how people take part in everyday life. Our approach is to find those barriers and encourage practical solutions: accessible infrastructure, skills, livelihood opportunities and community support.

Technology is also shaping the future of work. By encouraging rural youth to explore drone technology, semiconductor technology, electronics and digital learning, we help communities discover new pathways for education, careers and entrepreneurship.

Our journey is centred on empowering individuals, strengthening communities and encouraging a more accessible, inclusive and technologically aware society.

It is also a personal one. Our founder, Arivuraja, lost the use of both legs to polio at the age of five, studied computer science, and in 2020 started the drone-mapping company MAPe in Chennai. Today MAPe employs 120 people, four of them with disabilities, and he has trained more than 900 young people in Puducherry. Now he is offering free training, and jobs, to persons with disabilities who are interested in technology.

Vision and mission

Our vision

To create an accessible and inclusive society where persons with physical challenges enjoy independence, dignity and equal opportunities, while rural communities gain access to technology, skills and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Our mission

To promote accessible infrastructure, independent living, essential support, employment, entrepreneurship and technology education for persons with physical challenges, while bringing emerging technologies and future-oriented learning opportunities to rural youth.

We believe in abilities, not barriers.

We promote access, not limitations. We create opportunities for people to live independently, earn with dignity, learn new skills and build a better future.

Our values

Eight promises we hold ourselves to.

Dignity

Respecting every individual and recognising their potential.

Accessibility

Encouraging environments that people with different mobility requirements can access and use.

Inclusion

Promoting equal participation in society.

Independence

Supporting individuals in making their own choices and pursuing their aspirations.

Empowerment

Developing skills, confidence and opportunities.

Innovation

Exploring practical and technology-enabled approaches to community challenges.

Collaboration

Working with institutions, businesses, communities and volunteers.

Transparency

Maintaining accountability and responsible stewardship of resources.

Leadership with purpose

The social entrepreneur behind the Foundation, serving Kattumannarkoil since 2013.

A

Mr. T. Arivuraja

Founder and Managing Trustee

From Kattumannarkoil in Cuddalore district. Polio at the age of five affected both his legs. He completed a B.E. in Computer Science in 1995, worked in Chennai for seven years, and founded the drone-mapping company MAPe in 2020. It has 120 employees and has mapped for clients in 14 countries.

Through the National Skill Development scheme in Puducherry he has trained more than 900 young people. In 2026 he received a Mastery Award at the 24th CavinKare Ability Awards, which honour achievers with disabilities. More team members will be introduced here soon.
