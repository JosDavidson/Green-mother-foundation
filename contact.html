    Questions and answers

    Quick answers to what people ask us most. Cannot find yours? Use the form above.

    What is Green Mother Foundation’s primary objective?

    Our primary focus is to promote accessibility, independent living, employment, entrepreneurship and technology-enabled opportunities for persons with physical challenges.

    Does the Foundation promote ramp facilities?

    Yes. We aim to promote awareness of ramps, accessible entrances and barrier-free infrastructure, and to connect relevant stakeholders with appropriate accessibility initiatives.

    Can I request assistance from the Foundation?

    Yes. You can submit an enquiry through the website. The Foundation will review the request and respond according to its available programs and resources.

    Does the Foundation help with employment opportunities?

    We aim to facilitate career guidance, job-readiness support, skills development and connections with potential employers, depending on available programs and partnerships.

    Does the Foundation encourage entrepreneurship?

    Yes. We aim to promote self-employment, business awareness, livelihood development and access to relevant guidance and institutional resources.

    Why does the Foundation focus on drone technology?

    Drone technology has applications in surveying, mapping, agriculture, infrastructure and other fields. We aim to introduce rural youth to these applications and help them explore related learning and career pathways.

    What is the purpose of semiconductor technology awareness?

    The objective is to introduce rural youth to microchips, electronics, semiconductor fundamentals and related educational and career opportunities.

    Does the Foundation provide certified drone training?

    The Foundation aims to promote drone technology learning and awareness. Any formal flight training or certification will depend on the availability of suitably authorised training providers and applicable requirements.

    How can an educational institution collaborate with the Foundation?

    Schools, colleges, universities and training institutions can contact us to discuss technology awareness sessions, workshops, mentoring and community outreach.

    How can I become a volunteer?

    Complete the volunteer registration form on the website. The Foundation can review your interests, skills and availability.

    Can companies partner with the Foundation?

    Yes. Companies can enquire about accessibility initiatives, inclusive employment, technology education, skills development and suitable CSR collaborations.

    How can I support the Foundation?

    You may explore volunteering, institutional partnerships, technical mentoring, learning resources and other suitable forms of support.
