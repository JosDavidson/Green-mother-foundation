Measuring progress. Celebrating possibilities.

We aim for meaningful, practical and lasting results for individuals and communities. This page shares what we have done, what we have achieved and what we have learned, using verified information only.

Verified numbers only.

Every figure has a clear definition, a reporting period and a supporting record. We do not publish estimates as confirmed achievements.
