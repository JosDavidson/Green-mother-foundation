Creating opportunities. Transforming lives.

We believe every person has abilities, aspirations and the potential to achieve great things when given the right support.

Our vision is a society where persons with physical challenges live with dignity, independence and confidence, and where rural communities gain access to technology and sustainable livelihoods.

Real people. Real opportunities. Positive change.

From polio at five to 120 jobs

The challenge. Arivuraja lost the use of both legs to polio as a child, and went on to study computer science.

The initiative. In 2020 he founded MAPe, a drone-mapping company, and has trained over 900 young people in Puducherry.

The outcome. MAPe now employs 120 people, four of them with disabilities, and works in 14 countries.

Free training, and a job at the end of it

If you have a disability and a passion for technology, you can train with us free of charge, and we are ready to offer work.
