Accessibility statement

A foundation that works for accessibility must have a website that is accessible. This is how we build ours.

Our commitment

We test this website against recognised accessibility standards, including WCAG 2.2 level AA where feasible. We keep fixing what we find.

What we have built in

  • Keyboard-friendly navigation, with a focus ring you cannot miss
  • A page structure that works with screen readers
  • Descriptive alternative text for images
  • Clear form labels and error messages that say how to fix the problem
  • Enough contrast between text and background, at least 4.5 to 1
  • Captions for video where possible
  • Layouts that work on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops
  • Readable text, simple navigation, and buttons and links large enough to tap
  • A button to pause all background animation
  • An accessibility feedback form

Known limits

We are testing every page with keyboard, screen reader and zoom. So far we know of two limits: some older PDF reports are not fully tagged, and a few photos need longer descriptions. We aim to fix both by March 2027.

Tell us what is hard to use

If you find something hard to read, reach or use, please tell us. We read every message.

Privacy policy

We keep your information safe and use it only for the reason you gave it to us. The full policy will be written and reviewed before launch. It will cover:

  • Collecting only the information needed for the stated purpose
  • Asking for your consent in plain words
  • Limiting who can see enquiry and registration records
  • How long we keep information, and how we delete it
  • Protecting uploaded files and contact details
  • Handling forms securely

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to answer you, and we never sell them. You can ask us to see, correct or delete what we hold by writing to arivuraja@greenmotherfoundation.org.
