About this program

Accessibility helps people take part in education, employment, healthcare, public services and community life.

Green Mother Foundation aims to promote ramps, accessible entrances and barrier-free facilities that make buildings and public spaces easier for persons with physical challenges to reach.

Our goal

To promote safer, more convenient and independent access to everyday spaces.

What we plan to do

  • Identifying buildings and facilities with access barriers.
  • Running accessibility awareness programs.
  • Encouraging ramp facilities in public and private buildings.
  • Promoting accessible schools, colleges, workplaces, hospitals and community spaces.
  • Engaging with building owners, institutions and the relevant authorities.
  • Receiving accessibility requests and connecting people with the right stakeholders.
  • Encouraging awareness of accessible design and inclusive infrastructure.

Who it is for

People with physical challenges who find a building, entrance or public space hard to use.
Families and carers who know of an access barrier and want it fixed.
Building owners, schools, workplaces, hospitals and community spaces that want to become accessible.

    Questions and answers

    Does the Foundation promote ramp facilities?

    Yes. We aim to promote awareness of ramps, accessible entrances and barrier-free infrastructure, and to connect relevant stakeholders with appropriate accessibility initiatives.

    Can I request assistance from the Foundation?

    Yes. You can submit an enquiry through the website. The Foundation will review the request and respond according to its available programs and resources.

    What is Green Mother Foundation’s primary objective?

    Our primary focus is to promote accessibility, independent living, employment, entrepreneurship and technology-enabled opportunities for persons with physical challenges.
