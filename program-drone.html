Drone technology for rural youth

Bringing future technology to every village.

Register your interest in drone technologySee all programs

About this program

Drone technology is creating opportunities in agriculture, land surveying, mapping, infrastructure, environmental monitoring and many other sectors.

Green Mother Foundation aims to spread awareness of this technology among rural youth and help them explore the learning and career pathways it opens.

Our founder’s company, MAPe, uses drones to survey land, roads and buildings from above and turn them into precise digital data for railway, road, urban development and mining projects.

Our goal

To help rural youth discover technology-based learning and career opportunities.

Please note. Where regulated flight training or formal certification is involved, it will be delivered through appropriately authorised providers, in line with the applicable requirements.

Program areas

  • Drone awareness. An introduction to drone systems, their components and applications.
  • Technology demonstrations. A chance to see drone equipment and understand its practical uses.
  • Mapping and surveying. An introduction to aerial imagery, mapping and geospatial applications.
  • Technical fundamentals. Basic ideas about sensors, electronics, flight systems and data collection.
  • Career awareness. Information about drone-related roles, industry applications and further training.
  • Practical learning. Workshops and hands-on activities through suitable training institutions and qualified partners.
  • Industry connections. Chances to meet technology professionals and educational institutions.

Who it is for

Rural youth curious about drones and what they are used for.
Schools, colleges and training institutions that can host sessions.
Qualified, authorised providers of formal drone training.

    Questions and answers

    Why does the Foundation focus on drone technology?

    Drone technology has applications in surveying, mapping, agriculture, infrastructure and other fields. We aim to introduce rural youth to these applications and help them explore related learning and career pathways.

    Does the Foundation provide certified drone training?

    The Foundation aims to promote drone technology learning and awareness. Any formal flight training or certification will depend on the availability of suitably authorised training providers and applicable requirements.

    How can an educational institution collaborate with the Foundation?

    Schools, colleges, universities and training institutions can contact us to discuss technology awareness sessions, workshops, mentoring and community outreach.
