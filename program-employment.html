About this program

A person’s abilities, skills and determination should be at the centre of their career opportunities.

Green Mother Foundation aims to help persons with physical challenges explore suitable employment and connect with employers who value talent and performance.

Our goal

To strengthen connections between candidates and employers while encouraging accessible and supportive working environments.

What we plan to do

  • Career guidance.
  • Resume preparation.
  • Interview preparation.
  • Job-readiness training.
  • Digital and workplace skills.
  • Employer awareness programs.
  • Connections with potential employers.
  • Inclusive recruitment initiatives.
  • Guidance on accessible workplace practices.
  • Networking and career events.

Who it is for

Persons with physical challenges looking for suitable work.
Employers who value ability, talent and performance.
Recruitment organisations that want to hire inclusively.

    Questions and answers

    Does the Foundation help with employment opportunities?

    We aim to facilitate career guidance, job-readiness support, skills development and connections with potential employers, depending on available programs and partnerships.

    Can I request assistance from the Foundation?

    Yes. You can submit an enquiry through the website. The Foundation will review the request and respond according to its available programs and resources.

    Can companies partner with the Foundation?

    Yes. Companies can enquire about accessibility initiatives, inclusive employment, technology education, skills development and suitable CSR collaborations.
