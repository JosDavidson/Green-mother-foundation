About this program

Everyone should have the chance to take part in daily life as independently as possible.

The Foundation aims to help persons with physical challenges find information, resources and services that fit their needs.

Please note. We review all requests according to available capacity, the nature of the request and any eligibility requirements.

How we can help

Depending on available resources and partnerships, support may include:

  • Guidance on relevant government welfare schemes and services.
  • Information on applicable entitlements and support programs.
  • Referrals for mobility aids and assistive equipment.
  • Information about accessible facilities and services.
  • Digital access and online service guidance.
  • Community awareness and family engagement.
  • Referrals to suitable organisations and service providers.

Who it is for

Persons with physical challenges looking for information, resources and services.
Families who want to understand what support is available.
Organisations and service providers we can refer people to.

    Questions and answers

    Can I request assistance from the Foundation?

    Yes. You can submit an enquiry through the website. The Foundation will review the request and respond according to its available programs and resources.

    What is Green Mother Foundation’s primary objective?

    Our primary focus is to promote accessibility, independent living, employment, entrepreneurship and technology-enabled opportunities for persons with physical challenges.

    How can I support the Foundation?

    You may explore volunteering, institutional partnerships, technical mentoring, learning resources and other suitable forms of support.
