Programs that open doors

Ten programs follow one path: identify the barrier, train, enable, empower, build. Choose the step you need, or start with a request for help and we will point you to the right one.

Request assistanceRegister your interest
Step 1: Identify

Find the barrier and the need.

Where the path begins.

Accessibility and ramp facilities

Ramps, accessible entrances and barrier-free facilities for buildings and public spaces.

Learn more about accessibility
Step 2: Train

Build skills and know-how.

Learning for people with physical challenges and for rural youth.

Digital skills and technology education

Computer, internet and office skills, taught in workshops and online.

Learn more about digital skills

Drone technology for rural youth

Drone awareness, demonstrations and career pathways for young people in villages.

Learn more about drone technology

Semiconductor and electronics awareness

Microchips, circuits and electronics, introduced through hands-on learning.

Learn more about electronics
Step 3: Enable

Connect people with opportunity.

From skills to a job.

Employment and career development

Career guidance, job readiness and introductions to employers who value talent.

Learn more about employment
Step 4: Empower

Support independence and confidence.

For individuals, young people, women and rural communities.

Independent living and essential support

Information, referrals and guidance that strengthen confidence and independence.

Learn more about independent living

Rural youth empowerment

Technology camps, career guidance and industry interaction through community-based learning.

Learn more about rural youth

Youth empowerment

Confidence, communication, leadership and problem-solving for young people.

Learn more about youth

Women empowerment

Learning, vocational skills and livelihoods, with equal access for women.

Learn more about women
Step 5: Build

Grow a livelihood or a business.

The end of the path, and a new beginning.

Entrepreneurship and livelihood development

Business ideas, planning and mentors for self-employment and small businesses.

Learn more about entrepreneurship

Creating greater impact together

Partnerships help us reach further, teach better and keep programs going. They can be built around accessibility, employment, training, technology awareness, community engagement and livelihood development.
